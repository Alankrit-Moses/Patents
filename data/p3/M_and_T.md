# P3 — Causal chain

## M

Let V be a set of events, states, actions, or conditions, and let C(a,b) denote that a causes or contributes to b. A causal chain is an ordered sequence of distinct elements (v_1, v_2, ..., v_k), k >= 2, such that C(v_i,v_{i+1}) holds for every i = 1,...,k-1.

## T

A causal chain is a sequence of events, states, actions, or conditions in which each element causes or contributes to the next. It can contain a single cause-and-effect link or several connected links. The pattern applies across domains and can describe beneficial, neutral, or adverse outcomes.
